## What is Trakure?

Trakure is a powerful workflow management system designed specifically for export manufacturing firms. If you're drowning in spreadsheets, losing track of where your production items are, or struggling to give international buyers accurate delivery timelines and production updates, Trakure is your solution.

Think of Trakure as your **single source of truth** for everything happening in your export manufacturing workflow - from the moment you receive an international order to when items are completed and ready for shipment to your overseas buyers.

## Key Features Deep Dive

### Workflow Customization

**What it does**: Tailor the system to match your exact process.

**How you use it**:

1. Start with default stages (Order Intake → Manufacturing → Quality Check → Customization → Finishing → Packaging)
2. Add, remove, or rename stages to match your workflow
3. Create sub-stages for detailed tracking
4. Reorder stages as your process evolves

**Real example**: A jewelry exporter might set up:

- **Manufacturing** → _Casting_, _Setting_, _Polishing_
- **Quality Check** → _Weight Check_, _Visual Inspection_, _Final Approval_
- **Customization** → _Engraving_, _Stone Setting_, _Custom Plating_

### Item Movement & Tracking

**What it does**: Move items through your workflow while maintaining complete visibility.

**How you use it**:

1. See all items in each stage via the sidebar navigation
2. Select items to move forward (individual or bulk)
3. System automatically records the movement with timestamp
4. Items appear in the next stage instantly for all team members

**Smart features**:

- **Quantity Management**: Move partial quantities (e.g., 50 out of 100 pieces)
- **Target Stage Selection**: Skip stages if needed or jump to specific sub-stages
- **Bulk Operations**: Move multiple items at once

### Rework Management

**What it does**: Handle quality issues and corrections professionally.

**How you use it**:

1. During quality check, identify items that need rework
2. Select the problematic items and quantities
3. Choose which previous stage they should return to
4. Add a clear reason for the rework
5. System tracks these items separately until they're corrected

**Benefits**:

- Clear audit trail of all quality issues
- Prevents good items from being held up by problematic ones
- Helps identify recurring problems in your process

### Order Management

**What it does**: Centralize all your order information and track progress.

**Key features**:

- **Order Dashboard**: Quick overview of all orders with status indicators
- **Customer Information**: Track buyer details and payment status
- **Item Breakdown**: See exactly what items are in each order
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual indicators show order completion percentage

### Team Permissions

**What it does**: Give your team the right level of access without compromising security.

**Owner capabilities**:

- Full access to all features
- Manage team members and permissions
- Customize workflows and stages
- Access financial information and billing
- Generate invoices and vouchers

**Worker capabilities** (customizable by Owner):

- View and move items through workflow
- Add remarks and upload images
- Handle rework operations
- View order and item details
- Download movement vouchers

**Permission categories you can control**:

- Workflow editing
- Item operations (view, move, add, delete)
- Order management
- Document generation and export
- Team management
- Settings access

### Composite Items

**What it does**: Manage complex products made of multiple components.

**Example**: A "Complete Phone Case Set" might include:

- Front Cover (2 pieces)
- Back Cover (1 piece)
- Screen Protector (1 piece)
- Cleaning Cloth (1 piece)

**How it works**:

1. Define composite items with their component SKUs
2. When you add a composite item to an order, Trakure automatically creates individual tracking for each component
3. Each component moves through the workflow independently
4. Dashboard shows overall completion percentage for the composite item

### Smart Reminders

**What it does**: Prevent stockouts and delays by reminding you to order materials.

**How you set it up**:

1. Define what packaging materials each order type needs
2. Set trigger points (e.g., "Remind me when items reach Finishing stage")
3. Configure lead times for different suppliers
4. Receive email reminders at the right time

**Example**: "Order velvet jewelry boxes now - Order #12345 items will reach packaging in 3 days, and your supplier needs 5 days lead time."

### Reporting & Analytics

**What it provides**:

- **Live Dashboard**: Real-time counts and status indicators
- **Movement Statistics**: Track how long items spend in each stage
- **Bottleneck Identification**: See which stages are causing delays
- **Export Reports**: Download filtered data for further analysis
- **Item History**: Complete timeline for any item

## User Interface Walkthrough

### Dashboard (Your Daily Command Center)

The first thing you see when you log in:

**Top Stats Cards**:

- **Active Orders**: Orders with items still in progress
- **Active Items**: Total item quantities being processed
- **Items in Rework**: Items needing attention (red if > 0)
- **Items Waiting > 7 Days**: Potential bottlenecks (orange if > 0)

**Charts & Insights**:

- Movement statistics showing trends
- Bottleneck analysis highlighting problematic stages
- Time-based analytics for performance tracking

### Sidebar Navigation

Your workflow stages appear as a collapsible tree:

```
📋 New Orders (15)
🔧 Manufacturing (45)
  ├── Cutting (12)
  ├── Assembly (20)
  └── Polishing (13)
✅ Quality Check (8)
🎨 Customization (22)
  ├── Engraving (8)
  └── Plating (14)
📦 Packaging (5)
```

Click any stage to see the items currently there.

### Item List Table

Shows all items in the selected stage:

- **SKU & Details**: Item identification and specifications
- **Order Information**: Which order this item belongs to
- **Quantity**: How many pieces are in this stage
- **Time in Stage**: How long items have been waiting
- **Actions**: Move forward, rework, view details, add remarks

### Item Details Modal

Complete information about any item:

- **Order Context**: Customer, order number, payment status
- **Specifications**: Dimensions, weight, materials, etc.
- **Current Status**: Stage, sub-stage, time in current location
- **Movement History**: Complete timeline with timestamps
- **Images**: Photos uploaded at various stages
- **Remarks**: Notes and instructions from team members
